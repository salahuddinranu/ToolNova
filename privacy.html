Privacy Policy

Last updated: Today

1. Your Data is Yours

Most tools on ToolNova run entirely in your web browser. When you use tools like the Image Compressor or PDF Converter, your files are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. AI Tools

For AI-powered tools, the text you provide is sent to our secure server providers (like Supabase and Groq) strictly for processing the request. We do not permanently store your text.

3. Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic cookies to remember your theme preference (Dark/Light mode). If we use analytics or advertising in the future, this policy will be updated.
