Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ToolNova.

Is ToolNova really free?

Yes. All tools are free to use without signup. Some advanced AI features may require a Pro plan in the future — but the core tools will always stay free.

Are my files safe?

Yes. Most tools process files locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. For AI tools, text is sent to our secure backend and not stored.

Do I need to sign up?

No. ToolNova works fully without any account. Signup is optional and only needed for premium features.

Does it work on mobile?

Absolutely. ToolNova is built mobile-first and works perfectly on Android, iPhone, tablets, laptops, and desktops.
