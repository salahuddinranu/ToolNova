🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Most ToolNova tools run entirely in your browser using modern web technology. Your files and data are never uploaded to our servers unless a specific tool explicitly says so. AI tools use server-side processing via Supabase — and we tell you when they do. We never store your files permanently. Ever.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ToolNova really free?

Yes. All tools are free to use without signup. Some advanced AI features may require a Pro plan in the future — but the core tools will always stay free.

Are my files safe?

Yes. Most tools process files locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. For AI tools, text is sent to our secure Supabase backend and not stored beyond the request.

Do I need to sign up?

No. ToolNova works fully without any account. Signup is optional and only needed for premium features.

Does it work on mobile?

Absolutely. ToolNova is built mobile-first and works on Android, iPhone, tablets, laptops and desktops.

How many tools are there?

We're actively building toward 200+ tools. Tools marked "Coming Soon" are being developed. We never fake functionality — if a tool isn't ready, it says so.
