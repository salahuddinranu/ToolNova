Cookie Policy

Last updated: Today

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences (like dark mode) and improve your experience.

How We Use Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: We use a local storage key to remember your Dark/Light mode preference.
  • Analytics (Future): We may use privacy-friendly analytics to see how many people visit the site.
  • Ads (Future): If we show ads (like Google AdSense), those third-party services may use cookies to show relevant ads.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
